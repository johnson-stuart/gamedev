Slash World

Idea so far: Magical shadow/ghost/alien that is an NPC that you encounter early in the game. They are a shade that become bound to you. You use them to attack and interact with certain things. Midway through, they sacrifice themselves to an overpowered boss to save you. They crystallise and shatter then you have to recover their pieces, piece them back together. Now you have to become stronger on your own. Then you face the thing that caused it as the final boss, using attacks together, becoming even more powerful. 

Setting
Current world city. There's an apocalyptic event, alien invasion, perhaps origin of this shade thing. The city isn't safe and now you need the help of this alien/shade to survive. The alien transforms into shapes of animals since it has been studying animals on the planet since it arrived on the planet by accident thousands of years ago. It signalled for its home planet but it got picked up by this rogue alien civilisation that is trying to find it and harness its power (have I seen this somewhere before...)
